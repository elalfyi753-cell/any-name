01 / The research question

One panel. One wax backplate.
Six different cooling setups.

THE QUESTION

How does aluminum-fin geometry inside a paraffin-wax backplate affect a solar panel's temperature and electrical power under identical illumination?

HYPOTHESIS / NOT A FINDING

Pin or perforated fins may help warm wax circulate.

The prediction is that they will produce the greatest peak-temperature reduction. It is a testable guess, not an established result.

02 / How the cooling idea works

Heat takes a
different route.

STEP 01

Panel warms

Light reaches the PV module. Part of the absorbed energy becomes heat.

STEP 02

Fins conduct

Aluminum inserts transfer heat away from the backplate and into the wax.

STEP 03

Wax stores heat

As paraffin melts, it absorbs energy. Cooling may fade after the wax has melted.

HOLD CONSTANT
  • Same panel, wiring, and fixed electrical load
  • Same light source, distance, tilt, and duration
  • Same wax batch, mass, backplate, and contact method
  • Match aluminum mass across fin shapes within 1%
  • Begin at the same panel and wax temperature
MEASURE + REPEAT
  • Log temperature, voltage, current, and irradiance every 1-2 minutes
  • Run for at least 90 minutes to capture the melt cycle
  • Repeat each setup for at least 3 trials
  • Cool and re-solidify the wax between trials

05 / How results will be read

Measure temperature
and power together.

ELECTRICAL POWERP = V × I

Voltage multiplied by current.

PEAK TEMPERATURE DROPΔT = Tbare − Tcooled

Compare the same sensor location and run point.

POWER GAIN(Pcooled − Pbare) / Pbare × 100

Report the average and spread across trials.

Fair-winner rule: if two designs differ by less than the variation between their trials, report that the results are too close to distinguish instead of forcing a ranking.
